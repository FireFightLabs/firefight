# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Firefight to be a project anyone can contribute to, whatever their experience, background, or job title. Everyone taking part in this project agrees to make that possible.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about the problem, not about each other.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This adds a query per row" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way. A review that finds a problem has done its job.
- Be patient with people who are new. Everyone was.
- Respect a decision once it is made, and say so plainly when you disagree with it.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, the community Slack, and anywhere someone is representing Firefight.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **conduct@firefight.app**. Reports go to the maintainers and stay private. Tell us what happened, where, and when, and we will get back to you.

If your report is about a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled by the others.

## What happens next

Maintainers will look into every report and respond in a way that fits what happened. That can be a private word, a public correction, temporary removal from a discussion, or a permanent ban from the project.

We will not name a reporter without their permission.
