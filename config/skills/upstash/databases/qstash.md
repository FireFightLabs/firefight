QStash delivers a message to its destination URL and counts it delivered when the destination answers with a 2xx status. Otherwise it retries until the message's retries run out, and the message is then failed and moved to the dead letter queue. The states a delivery log shows are CREATED, ACTIVE, RETRY, ERROR, DELIVERED, FAILED, CANCEL_REQUESTED and CANCELLED.

1. Call `search_errors` for the region's QStash over the trouble. Messages are grouped by destination and the status it answered last. One destination answering 5xx is that service failing, 401 or 403 is it refusing QStash, and no answer at all is a timeout or a destination that cannot be reached.
2. Call `search_logs` for the same QStash with `text` set to the destination for the delivery attempts, newest first, with each one's state, status and error. Retries spaced further and further apart are QStash backing off.
3. When messages are not sent at all on time, call `qstash_list_schedules` with the region for whether the schedule is paused and what its cron says, and `qstash_flow_control_get` for a rate or parallelism limit holding messages back.
4. For more than the capabilities show, such as one message's attempts, call `logs_list` or `dlq_list` with the region and service qstash.
5. When a schedule keeps sending to a destination that is down, so every run piles retries and dead letters onto it, propose pausing that schedule with `qstash_manage_schedule` while the destination recovers. Name the schedule by its id from `qstash_list_schedules`, say how often it runs and what it sends to, and that nothing it would have sent is caught up later. A pause is undone after the time the person sees unless they keep it, by resuming the same schedule with `qstash_manage_schedule`. Resume it once the destination answers again, and never delete a schedule to stop it.
6. Once the destination answers again, propose redelivering the failed messages. Call `dlq_list` with the region and service qstash for them, each with its dead letter id, destination and body. When `dlq_manage` offers retry, retry each by its dead letter id. When it does not, publish each again with `qstash_publish_message` as `dlq_list` shows it, with the same destination, method, headers, body and retries, and then delete it from the dead letter queue with `dlq_manage`, so it is not delivered twice later. A message whose settings the publish tool cannot carry over is left for a person to retry from the Upstash console, and the answer names it. Say how many messages, to which destination and since when, since the destination acts on each one again. Never deliver them while the destination still fails, since they only return to the dead letter queue.
7. Call `search_logs` for the QStash with `text` set to the destination afterward to see them delivered.
8. Say which destination failed, since when, with what answer, what was delivered again, and whether the destination or QStash's settings need to change.
