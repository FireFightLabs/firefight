## Key Insights

### 1. Pricing Models

**incident.io**: **Per-user subscription** (most transparent)
- Scales linearly
- Predictable costs
- Easy to understand

**FireHydrant**: **Tiered by responder count** (least transparent)
- Fixed tiers with user limits
- Must upgrade at thresholds
- Less predictable scaling

**Rootly**: **Volume-based tiers** (middle ground)
- Discount at higher volumes
- Negotiable pricing
- Better for larger teams

### 2. Hidden Costs

**incident.io**:
- ⚠️ On-call is separate (+$10-20/user)
- ⚠️ True cost is $29-45/user, not $19-25/user

**FireHydrant**:
- ⚠️ Signals (alerting) charged separately
- ⚠️ SMS/phone alerts limited (50/month)
- ⚠️ Integration limits (5 on Pro)

**Rootly**:
- ⚠️ On-call is separate product
- ⚠️ Workflow automations limited on Essentials

### 3. Free Tier Comparison

| Feature | incident.io | FireHydrant | Rootly |
|---------|------------|-------------|---------|
| **Users** | 5 | 10 (2 weeks) | Limited |
| **Duration** | Forever | Trial only | Forever |
| **On-call** | 1 schedule | Yes | No |
| **Integrations** | 2 | 3 | Unlimited |
| **Workflows** | 1 | 2 runbooks | Limited |
| **API** | ❌ | ❌ | ? |

**Winner**: Rootly (unlimited integrations, forever free)

### 4. Mid-Market Sweet Spot (10-50 users)

**Cheapest**: Rootly (~$200-1,000/month)
**Most Expensive**: FireHydrant (~$400-2,000/month)
**Middle**: incident.io ($290-1,450/month)

### 5. Enterprise (100+ users)

**Cheapest**: incident.io ($2,900/month at 100 users)
**Most Expensive**: FireHydrant (~$5,000/month estimated)
**Middle**: Rootly ($3,500/month at 100 users)
